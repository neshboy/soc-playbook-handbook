# Visual Inventory

This document catalogs every diagram and chart produced for the SOC Playbook Handbook. Ground truth for the **Status** column was established by checking the actual filesystem contents of `assets/diagrams/` and `assets/charts/` on 2026-09-15, not by trusting the generating agent's self-reported check results.

**Result of the filesystem audit: all 50 expected diagrams and all 12 expected charts are present on disk.** Several charts that were self-reported as "NO RESULT / agent may have failed" (mtta-trend, mttr-trend, alert-volume-before-after-tuning, repeat-incident-rate, analyst-rework-rate) were in fact found on disk and are marked Present below.
